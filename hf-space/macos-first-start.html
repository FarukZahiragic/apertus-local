First start on a Mac

Use an Apple M-series Mac with macOS 15 or newer. We recommend 16 GB RAM. No Apple Developer membership or build tools are needed.

  1. Extract the ZIP. Move Apertus Local.app into Applications or another permanent folder, then double-click it.

  2. Approve the app if macOS blocks it. This preview has no Apple-verified publisher or notarization. After trying to open it, dismiss the warning, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, and choose Open Anyway for Apertus Local. Confirm the next prompt. Your Mac may ask for your login password or Touch ID.

  3. Allow Terminal access when asked. The app opens a Terminal window to show progress. Keep that window open while chatting.

  4. Get the model once. The launcher downloads and checks the 5.3 GiB model, then opens local chat in your browser. Stay online until preparation finishes.

  5. Chat locally. After preparation, you can restart and chat with networking off. Press Control+C in Terminal to stop. Open the app again to start another session.

Approve only the app you intentionally downloaded from this project's release page. Keep system-wide security checks enabled. If a managed Mac does not offer an approval button, ask its administrator. A warning about malware or a damaged app requires help from the organizer.
