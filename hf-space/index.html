FROM ONE COMMAND TO FIRST ANSWER

Prepare once. Then you can disconnect.

  1. 01

    Paste the command

    Open Terminal or PowerShell and paste the command above. Keep its window open while you chat.

  2. 02

    Get the model once

    The launcher downloads and verifies missing files. Existing model files can be reused. At a workshop, you can copy the same model from USB.

  3. 03

    Chat on your laptop

    Your browser opens local chat. Use the saved launcher to restart with networking off and check your setup. Close with Ctrl+C in the terminal.

WHAT RUNS WHERE

The website is the doorway.
Your laptop does the work.

This page helps you download the software. The native runtime runs the model and serves chat at 127.0.0.1:8080. The chat interface and model are available locally after setup.

Paste text into chat for document questions. This distribution contains the text model; image and audio inputs are not included.
