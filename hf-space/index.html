A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Before you start

Model files & checksums ↗

Which laptops are supported?

Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 13.3 or later, 64-bit Windows 10 or later on Intel/AMD or ARM, and compatible Intel/AMD Linux systems. We recommend 16 GB RAM; 8 GB machines are not yet validated. Leave room for the model, runtime, and future downloads. Full system requirements ↗

How do I open it again?

Setup prints the saved launcher path. On Windows, use Start.cmd and Stop.cmd. On macOS or Linux, run the saved start.sh command in Terminal and press Ctrl+C to stop. Keep the terminal window open while chatting.

Can I use photos and audio?

Yes. Attach a clear photo or a short audio clip in local chat; Apertus answers in text. Start with less than 15 seconds of speech. Your laptop processes the attachments, so response times depend on your hardware.

Where are my conversations processed?

On your laptop, in the local chat page opened by the launcher. This website provides downloads; it does not receive your conversations or attachments. Internet is needed for initial setup, then you can disconnect.
